# Roadmap

> From PLAN.md ¤11 (the full M0–M31 table there is the source of truth ‒ this page tracks status only). M0 shipped `fd620a0` ‒ island terrain, instanced vegetation, 2.5D camera, player rig, day/night. M1 shipped `f2f6e6d` ‒ survival stats, water, fire+cooking, coconuts, non-lethal spear + fish, crabs, death/respawn; see [[m1-survival-systems]]. M2 shipped ‒ camp spot selection (`H`, validity-checked), fixed-offset shelter/bed structures (`J`/`K`), camp-aware respawn; see [[m2-camp-system]]. M3 shipped ‒ camp fence (`L`, entrance facing the stored heading) + standing camp torch (`N`), `campIsLit()` (torch or fire within 8 m), HUD lit state; see [[m3-camp-fence-light]]. M4 shipped `9d96547` ‒ sleep (`Z` at a lit camp bed): clock jumps to dawn, resting stats at half rate, rest restores +30 health, wake on the bed; see [[m4-sleep-system]]. M5 shipped `98569cc` ‒ first tiger: 3 deterministic jungle-band spawns, hide/patrol/stalk/chase/flee FSM, contact strike (‒30 health + all loot dropped), any lit fire or camp torch drives tigers off; see [[m5-tiger]]. M6 shipped `4e30d98` ‒ night-1 encounter: scripted first-night tiger beat (~13 m close on first dusk, fire/torch drives it off), minimal gulls (M14 placeholder: one-food steal, telegraphed dive-bomb, water-shark scare tell), 3 extra night crabs; see [[m6-night-1-encounter]]. M7 shipped `abfe70a` ‒ vegetation framework: one `PLANT_TYPES` data row per species (M8/M9 extend the rows, not world logic), per-species private `mulberry32(SEED+offset)` spawn (global world-gen rng untouched), game-minute-driven growth/state (sleep + clock-skips fast-forward for free), `E` harvest of fiber; see [[m7-vegetation-framework]]. M8 shipped `d1594ef` ‒ edible + poisonous foraging: 4 forage edibles + 3 color-shift lookalike poisons (identification is learned from eating, never labeled), `Q` eats by risk/reward order (poisons only under hunger pressure), vomit/real-second drain poison effects, map-driven fire cook (M1 priority kept); see [[m8-forage-foods]]. M9 shipped `742bfa1` ‒ medicinal plants + harvesting/regrowth: healing herb (medicine, not food ‒ `M` chews it, `Q` never eats it), tiger-strike persistent infection bleed cured only by the herb, per-spot deterministic regrow wear (over-harvesting slows regrowth); see [[m9-medicinal-plants]]. M10 shipped `f55a987` ‒ weather framework: clear/cloudy/rain as a seeded, lazily-extended schedule keyed on elapsed `gameMin` (private rng stream, survives clock jumps/rewinds, data-driven `WEATHER_NEXT` so M11 appends `storm`), visible sky/fog/sun/rain-streak blend + HUD, rain douses unsheltered fires (shelter r2.0 protects; camp torches rain-proof; a doused cook keeps its raw item), rain-accelerated plant growth owns the M7 hook (range-average `plantGrowthMult`), `?weather=` debug only; see [[m10-weather-framework]]. M11 shipped `34a064e` ‒ storm gameplay: `storm` appended as the 4th schedule state (only follows rain, 45–150 min, heavy rain for dousing/growth), per-storm precomputed lightning strikes + wind coconut drops on a private per-segment rng stream (smoke replays every value), strikes ignite open-ground fires, sear unsheltered players, and panic tigers/gulls/crabs, new `wet` stat (soak in rain/storm, dry by shelter or fire, hp drain when soaking), storm refuses the spear (lagoon surge), rain masks fire/smoke (tigers fear fires less), `?weather=storm` debug only; see [[m11-storm-gameplay]]. M12 shipped `c6ade32` ‒ animal framework: shared `animals[]` registry + behaviour helpers (`homeWander`/`returnHome`/`faceHome`/`applyMove`) + early/late frame phases; the 4 existing species re-registered with unchanged behaviour, new species join with no bespoke world wiring; see [[m12-animal-framework]]. M13 shipped `e85ab49` ‒ land wildlife: monkeys (jungle-edge meat stealer, tiger-proximity shriek tell, coconut bait), large lizards (tide-line crab hunter, dusk camp raid held out by fence AND fire), snakes (reactive-only ambush, 0.8 s telegraph, venomous/harmless split); registry 4 -> 7, `frame()` untouched; see [[m13-land-wildlife]]. M14 shipped `6665b91` ‒ beach/air wildlife: 3 pelicans (registry slot 7, the last; palm-crown perches, deep-dive fish finder, 1.2 s hover steal countered by Space), gull cliff nest with 3 stealable eggs (theft = one visible day of 8 s-cadence dive-bombing), gull mob of a pelican's dive point, crabs 9 -> 15 with the hide-from-lizard tell; registry 7 -> 8, `frame()` untouched; see [[m14-beach-air-wildlife]]. M15 shipped `567fd76` ‒ sharks + expanded fish ecosystem: 2 never-killable reef sharks (registry slot 8, `'early'`: patrol/curious/bite FSM with 0.7 s lunge telegraph, in-water-only bites, festering-wound blood draw, storm-bold, aggressive dawn/dusk, gull-scream tell) + fish-school slow regeneration (1/120 s, capped) and dawn/dusk double-bite spear; registry 8 -> 9, `frame()` untouched; see [[m15-sharks-fish-ecosystem]]. M16 shipped `8532b15` ‒ animal alarm bus + cross-species reactions: a small deterministic `alarms[]` bus (`alarmRaise`/`alarmsNear`/`updateAlarms`, one decay per frame in `frame()` before both animal phases, no rng, server world-sync safe) where the six tell moments emit (`scream`: monkey shriek, curious-shark edge, gull dive; `attack`: shark bite, tiger strike; `alert`: `panicWildlife`) and six species react (crabs bolt from every alarm, monkeys freeze facing the alarm, lizards scare-flee, snakes freeze, pelicans flee, fish schools scatter) + 2 new tells (`crabalarm`, `fishscatter`); all reactions additive, registry 9 unchanged, all M12–M15 asserts hold; see [[m16-animal-alarm-bus]].  M17 shipped — animal ecosystem integration + balancing: the spider joins as the 10th species (registry slot 9, `'late'`, pop 3; night-only, non-lethal, creeps into an unlit camp near the player and snipes one leftover meat item after a 0.9 s clicking tell; a lit fire or torch is the counter, no fence needed; alarm-bus reaction only) and the 10-species coexistence sweep (constant-knob tuning only, populations stay pinned, spawns strictly after the shark block keep the M12–M15 rng stream intact); registry 9 -> 10, `frame()` untouched, cave blind spiders + silk resource deferred (no cave terrain; crafting is M20); see [[m17-spider-ecosystem-integration]]. M18 shipped `e8f9270` ‒ paper map system: no minimap, key `Y` opens a parchment sheet drawn only as far as walked (visited-cell grid over a one-shot deterministic terrain raster, no rng); within 40 u, landmarks (wreck from the start, camp+flag, fires, gull nest, school fishing spots, plant sites) and per-instance animal territories (all 10 species) are recorded once, forever; pure player-side runtime state like journal/learned ‒ no world mutation, no rng, no saving (M25 owns saves); `frame()` gains only `mapTick` + throttled `mapDraw`, closes on death, `?map=1` debug only; see [[m18-paper-map-system]]. M19 shipped `8c23a63` ‒ journal + discoveries: the M6 toast-only journal became a viewable field journal (`I`, parchment overlay, four categories, "N more to learn" from a static still-to-learn catalog), entries are structured `{cat, msg, day}` with `day` derived from the game clock (never counted), and first-sighting discoveries record `see-<id>` once per species within 30 u; player-side state only ‒ no rng, no world mutation, no saving (M25 owns saves); `frame()` gains only `journalTick`; see [[m19-journal-discoveries]]. M20 shipped `0ab4f16` ‒ raft construction: the complete gather/craft/build path for the winning raft ‒ wood is a new `INV` counter gathered from spent palms (`cocos == 0` palm shake, coconut drop + storm wind untouched), rope is the first real recipe (`U`, 3 fiber tufts anywhere), and the raft is a camp structure (`O`, within 10 u of camp: 8 wood + 4 rope) placed at a new forward `campOffsets('raft')` slot inside the fence ring; `INV` resets at both death sites, M19 journal gains the `SURVIVAL_CATALOG` survival line, zero world-gen/`PLANT_TYPES`/animal-registry changes; see [[m20-raft-construction]]. M21 shipped `d312b0c` ‒ escape/win sequence: the completed M20 raft is now a working game ending ‒ `E` boards/unboards the raft (2.5 u, `learn('sail')`), aboard `frame()` rows the raft itself at 4.0/6.0 u/s past the island clamp/ground-follow, and rowing past `WIN_R = ISLAND_R + 60` (270 u, beyond max shark reach ~260) triggers `winEscape()`; `won`/`started=false` freeze the sim through the existing gates while the renderer keeps drawing, and the `#won` overlay (mirrors `#dead`: days ashore, discoveries, animals seen) ends the run with `R` to restart; the raft is shark-uncounterable, keydown is `won`-guarded, zero world-gen/`PLANT_TYPES`/animal-registry changes; see [[m21-escape-win]]. M22 shipped `d421394` ‒ endless mode + adaptation: after the M21 win overlay `E` stays on the island (`startEndless()` flips endless/won/started, rig back at camp, raft kept built so the player stays re-escapable, `learn('endless')` + journal line); the island then escalates per endless day via a small boost family that is all 0 in story mode (story stays bit-identical) ‒ tiger boost 0.12/day cap 0.5 (stalk/chase ranges, speeds, attack cd; damage unchanged), pelican boost 0.15/day cap 0.6 (steal radius/window/cd), day boost 0.04/day cap 0.5 through `timeRate` (days up to +50% shorter), storm boost cap 1.0 (storm weight + duration via the same single weather rng draw ‒ no new draws); score = days survived (HUD line + endless death overlay, camp respawn with carried loot lost); only tiger + pelican escalate, zero world-gen/`PLANT_TYPES`/animal-registry changes; M23 shipped `4bb4e83` ‒ audio pass: procedural WebAudio, generated only (no assets) ‒ one self-contained section in `index.html` + an `AudioContext` stub in `smoke/run.mjs`; four cue families (island ambience beds, weather beds tracking `weather.vis.rain`/`.storm` + nearest-fire crackle, 10 distinct animal signatures + alarm-bus backstop, survival-action cues + zone footsteps), `audioInit()` on first keydown (autoplay policy), read-only `audioTick(dt)` re-deriving bed gains per frame (sleep-jump/T-skip safe), 40 u distance gate + range-delayed thunder, `P` mute, zero `rng()`/`Math.random` (pure clock hashes); see [[m23-audio]]. `node smoke/run.mjs` → `SMOKE_OK`.
