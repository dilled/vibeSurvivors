M6 shipped `4e30d98` ‒ night-1 encounter: scripted first-night tiger beat (~13 m close on first dusk, fire/torch drives it off), minimal gulls (M14 placeholder: one-food steal, telegraphed dive-bomb, water-shark scare tell), 3 extra night crabs; see [[m6-night-1-encounter]]. M7 shipped `abfe70a` ‒ vegetation framework: one `PLANT_TYPES` data row per species (M8/M9 extend the rows, not world logic), per-species private `mulberry32(SEED+offset)` spawn (global world-gen rng untouched), game-minute-driven growth/state (sleep + clock-skips fast-forward for free), `E` harvest of fiber; see [[m7-vegetation-framework]]. M8 shipped `d1594ef` ‒ edible + poisonous foraging: 4 forage edibles + 3 color-shift lookalike poisons (identification is learned from eating, never labeled), `Q` eats by risk/reward order (poisons only under hunger pressure), vomit/real-second drain poison effects, map-driven fire cook (M1 priority kept); see [[m8-forage-foods]]. M9 shipped `742bfa1` ‒ medicinal plants + harvesting/regrowth: healing herb (medicine, not food ‒ `M` chews it, `Q` never eats it), tiger-strike persistent infection bleed cured only by the herb, per-spot deterministic regrow wear (over-harvesting slows regrowth); see [[m9-medicinal-plants]]. M10 shipped `f55a987` ‒ weather framework: clear/cloudy/rain as a seeded, lazily-extended schedule keyed on elapsed `gameMin` (private rng stream, survives clock jumps/rewinds, data-driven `WEATHER_NEXT` so M11 appends `storm`), visible sky/fog/sun/rain-streak blend + HUD, rain douses unsheltered fires (shelter r2.0 protects; camp torches rain-proof; a doused cook keeps its raw item), rain-accelerated plant growth owns the M7 hook (range-average `plantGrowthMult`), `?weather=` debug only; see [[m10-weather-framework]]. M11 shipped `34a064e` ‒ storm gameplay: `storm` appended as the 4th schedule state (only follows rain, 45–150 min, heavy rain for dousing/growth), per-storm precomputed lightning strikes + wind coconut drops on a private per-segment rng stream (smoke replays every value), strikes ignite open-ground fires, sear unsheltered players, and panic tigers/gulls/crabs, new `wet` stat (soak in rain/storm, dry by shelter or fire, hp drain when soaking), storm refuses the spear (lagoon surge), rain masks fire/smoke (tigers fear fires less), `?weather=storm` debug only; see [[m11-storm-gameplay]]. M12 shipped `c6ade32` ‒ animal framework: shared `animals[]` registry + behaviour helpers (`homeWander`/`returnHome`/`faceHome`/`applyMove`) + early/late frame phases; the 4 existing species re-registered with unchanged behaviour, new species join with no bespoke world wiring; see [[m12-animal-framework]]. M13 shipped `e85ab49` ‒ land wildlife: monkeys (jungle-edge meat stealer, tiger-proximity shriek tell, coconut bait), large lizards (tide-line crab hunter, dusk camp raid held out by fence AND fire), snakes (reactive-only ambush, 0.8 s telegraph, venomous/harmless split); registry 4 -> 7, `frame()` untouched; see [[m13-land-wildlife]]. M14 shipped `6665b91` ‒ beach/air wildlife: 3 pelicans (registry slot 7, the last; palm-crown perches, deep-dive fish finder, 1.2 s hover steal countered by Space), gull cliff nest with 3 stealable eggs (theft = one visible day of 8 s-cadence dive-bombing), gull mob of a pelican's dive point, crabs 9 -> 15 with the hide-from-lizard tell; registry 7 -> 8, `frame()` untouched; see [[m14-beach-air-wildlife]]. M15 shipped `567fd76` ‒ sharks + expanded fish ecosystem: 2 never-killable reef sharks (registry slot 8, `'early'`: patrol/curious/bite FSM with 0.7 s lunge telegraph, in-water-only bites, festering-wound blood draw, storm-bold, aggressive dawn/dusk, gull-scream tell) + fish-school slow regeneration (1/120 s, capped) and dawn/dusk double-bite spear; registry 8 -> 9, `frame()` untouched; see [[m15-sharks-fish-ecosystem]].
